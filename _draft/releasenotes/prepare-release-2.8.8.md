# Classic Era / The Burning Crusade

## Bug Fixes
### General
Effects which rely on talents sometimes detected incorrectly which talent was picked. This bug was introduced in version 2.6.0. All versions between 2.6.0 and 2.8.7 (included) are subject to this issue.
### Rogue
Riposte could glow without the talent. This could happen if the Rogue performed the following actions in this exact order
1. Pick the Riposte talent
2. Put the Riposte button to an action bar
3. Forget talents (respec) and not pick Riposte again
4. Keep the Riposte button ni the action bar (it will be grayed out)
5. Enter combat and parry the enemy: Riposte may glow, which is a bug
## Contributors
Shout-out to our amazing contributors
- zzcbun for reporting the talent issue
