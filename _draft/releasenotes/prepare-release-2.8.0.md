# Forever

## General
### Game Updates
The addon introduces a new flavor: Forever.

Forever support is not final at the moment, hence the _alpha_ designation, for now. Please read the Known Limitations section for more information.

Here is the full list of supported Spell Alerts so far
- Druid's Nature's Grace
- Druid's Omen of Clarity
- Hunter's Mongoose Bite
- Mage's Clearcasting
- Priest's Focused Casting (Martyrdom)
- Rogue's Riposte
- Shaman's Elemental Focus
- Warlock's Eye of Kilrogg
- Warlock's Nightfall (Shadow Trance)

And here is the list of Glowing Buttons
- Hunter's Counterattack
- Hunter's Mongoose Bite
- Paladin's Hammer of Wrath
- Paladin's Holy Shock
- Paladin's Exorcism
- Rogue's Riposte
- Warrior's Overpower
- Warrior's Execute
- Warrior's Revenge

**There will be a new release every day until the end of the Forever Beta, on October 21st**. If your favorite effect is not on the list, there is a decent chance that it will be supported in the near future. Either way, **if you want to have the best experience possible, your are welcome to come over the Discord server or CurseForge and send feedback**. It can be anything from reporting issues to suggesting new effects. Don't be shy! The more the feedback is shared, the better the addon will be!
## Known Limitations
### Forever
Glowing Buttons are not supported for auras e.g, Shadow Bolt for Nightfall
- It is unsure whether Glowing Buttons can become a reality with auras
- Non-auras Glowing Buttons, such as Warrior's Execute, do work

Most Spell Alerts do not support Progressive Timer
- Some progressive timer alternatives are being evaluated
- Support will definitely come someday, but will be different than Classic

Most Spell Alerts cannot have different visuals based on number of stacks
- Because of that, some effects have been postponed
- Alternatives are being evaluated

Most Spell Alerts do not support Sound Effects
- Some sound alternatives are being evaluated
- If support is possible, it will be probably similar to Classic's sound

Custom effects are not supported
- This includes things like Mage's Freeze effect
- There is no ETA on this matter, as it requires further investigation

Non-exclusive execute-like effects are not supported
- 'Non-exclusive execute' means a spell that can be cast at any time
- This includes things like Priest's Shadow Word: Death
- Excusive execute, such as Warrior's Execute, are correctly supported

Some effects are supported only outside dungeons and raids
- For example, Paladin's Exorcism will _not_ work in a dungeon or raid
- This is a limitation wanted by Blizzard and unlikely to change, sorry

For some Spell Alerts disabling them from the Options Panel has no effect
- On the other hand, disabling a Glowing Button always work

Druid's Omen of Clarity may not work right after learning Cat/Bear form
- This is a one-time issue happening when finishing their respective quests
- Simply logging out and logging back in fixes the issue

Stance-based visuals may display a short ghost when switching stance
- Druid's Omen of Clarity may trigger that issue when visuals go red/green
- The issue does not trigger if the stance never changes during the effect

_Developer's Note: The above list can be daunting, but some issues aren't noticeable to most players, while others are being fixed as we speak. This list is here to be 100% transparent about where development is going. It also helps players who have issues anwsering the question "should I report this issue?". **If you're experiencing an issue which is not on the list, by all means, please report the it**. Thank you :)_
## Miscellaneous
This release uses a new technology to detect auras
- This increases performance and has better long-term support
- All flavors should be compatible with this technology as of today
- Don't worry, this has been tested internally for almost a year now :)
