### :druid:  Druid

### Options
Opening the options panel is delayed after leaving combat (Forever, Era, The Burning Crusade, Wrath of the Lich King).

_Developer's Note: This delay is annoying and became mandatory with Siege of Orgrimmar patch for Mists of Pandaria Classic, and is now mandatory with pretty much all flavors of Classic and with Forever. The one exception is Cataclysm Classic, just because there is currently no official game for Cataclysm Classic._
