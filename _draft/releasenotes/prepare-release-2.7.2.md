# Classic Era

## Classes
## Shaman
Elemental Focus visuals are now consistent across expansions and across Clearcasting effects in general.

_Developer's Note: Visuals of Classic Era are now on par with Cataclysm. Some Shamans may feel that the new visuals are now a bit dull on these flavors. On the other hand, Shamans playing across several flavors should welcome this change, because visuals will be much less confusing when switching between expansions._
## Miscellaneous
This release optimizes code size a bit further than in 2.7.0.


# The Burning Crusade

## Classes
### Shared
Healing Trance visuals have been updated.

_Developer's Note: Previously, Healing Trance shared visuals with other Clearcasting effects. Therefore, it was not easy to identify which effect was triggered. New visuals should be different enough to indicate which visuals belong to which buff, yet they should be close enough to know that their effects are related i.e., they lower the mana cost of the next spell cast._
### Priest
New Spell Alerts
- Clearcasting

New Glowing Buttons
- Binding Heal, during Clearcasting
- Flash Heal, during Clearcasting
- Greater Heal, during Clearcasting
## Shaman
New Spell Alerts
- Shaman's Shamanistic Focus\*

New Glowing Buttons
- Shaman's Earth Shock, during Shamanistic Focus\*
- Shaman's Flame Shock, during Shamanistic Focus\*
- Shaman's Frost Shock, during Shamanistic Focus\*

\* Shamanistic Focus is disabled by default and must be enabled from the options panel: `/sao`

Elemental Focus visuals are now consistent across expansions and across Clearcasting effects in general.

_Developer's Note: Visuals of The Burning Crusade and Wrath are now on par with Cataclysm. Some Shamans may feel that the new visuals are now a bit dull on these flavors. On the other hand, Shamans playing across several flavors should welcome this change, because visuals will be much less confusing when switching between expansions._
## Bug Fixes
### Glowing Buttons
Buttons should glow as intended with Dominos action bars.
## Miscellaneous
This release optimizes code size a bit further than in 2.7.0.
## Contributors
Shout-out to our amazing contributors
- Nezzey and @catpokemon for suggesting Priest's Clearcasting effect
- simgr0 for suggesting Shaman's Shamanistic Focus
- cottycotty for reporting the Dominos issue
Thanks!

# Retail

## General
### Game Updates
This release is a quick experiment that scratches the surface of Retail.
- **No effects are supported**
- **Many Lua errors can happen**
The goal is merely to see how an empty shell works around Retail.

If there is enough interest from players, and if the Midnight addonpocalypse does not strike this addon too hard, there might be more updates coming to Retail.

As always, feedback is appreciated, thank you :)
