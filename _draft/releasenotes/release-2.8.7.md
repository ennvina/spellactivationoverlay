## Bug Fixes
### Spell Alerts
Spell Alert sounds no longer play for minor effects (Forever)
### :druid:  Druid
Omen of Clarity option now works in Cat/Bear form (Forever). More exactly, it was possible to enable/disable the Omen of Clarity option, but that affected only the Humanoid form. The spell alert would always show up when the effect triggered in Cat and Bear form, even when the option was disabled. Now, disabling the option will disable both the 'green' and 'red' spell alerts, as intended.
### :mage~1:  Mage
Previewing Fingers of Frost now displays 2 stacks instead of 1 (Forever). This issue happened to the preview functionality only, in the Options Panel. In combat, Fingers of Frost already displayed up to 2 stacks as intended.

Heating Up and Hot Streak should trigger less often on false positives (Wrath of the Lich King). It is unsure whether these false positives actually happened, but this release should prevent them from happening.
