# SpellActivationOverlay 2.8.5
@here I am pleased to announce the newest release of SpellActivationOverlay, version 2.8.5
## Classes
### :mage~1:  Mage
New Spell Alert
- Heating Up (Forever)
Updated Spell Alerts
- Missile Barrage has been scaled up and layered behind Fingers of Frost (Forever)

The goal is to tackle the potential overlap between Missile Barrage and Fingers of Frost, which became possible in last patch.
## Bug Fixes
### General
Effects are more consistent when a buff is gained and lost exactly at the same time (Classic Era, The Burning Crusade, Wrath of the Lich King, Cataclysm, Mists of Pandaria). It meant either gaining the buff then losing it immediately, or the other way around.

While in theory it could happen to any effect based on a buff, in practice it happened only for a handful of effects, such as Mage's Heating Up.
### :mage~1:  Mage
Heating Up should no longer cancel earlier than intended (Mists of Pandaria)
