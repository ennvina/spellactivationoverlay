## First round of supported class effects (pre-alpha)

Here is the list of supported effects for Forever, from the first branch that tries to support Forever i.e., retail-before-forever.

At first, the goal is to **support at least the ones that exist in Classic Era**, unless either
- The effect changed in Forever and it no longer makes sense to track it
- There are technical difficulties to support it

Please report issues you may have, for example
- The effect is not listed in the Options Panel `/sao`
- The Spell Alert or the Glowing Button is not activated when the effect procs
- Visuals look weird (wrong texture, wrong location, wrong animations, etc.)
- Warning/errors are displayed in the chatbox or in the game's error console
- …or pretty much anything that seems off
